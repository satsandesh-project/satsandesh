# Elder Interview Guide — SatSandesh Month 1 Research

**Purpose:** Structured guide for conducting elder user interviews to inform
the SRS and UX design of SatSandesh. Each session should take 30–45 minutes.
Conduct sessions in the elder's home or a familiar, quiet setting whenever
possible. Have a family member present if it helps the elder feel at ease,
but ask them to stay silent during tasks so observations reflect the elder's
own ability.

## Observed Patterns in Elder Mobile Phone Usage
*(Reference section — drawn from prior literature and team observations;
update as interviews add new evidence)*

### Common strengths
- Highly motivated by family contact — tasks with a clear social purpose
  are completed with greater persistence
- Voice messages preferred over typing across almost all literacy levels
- WhatsApp familiarity is nearly universal in urban/semi-urban India (65+);
  its patterns can be used as a baseline mental model

### Common pain points
- **Touch target size** — buttons smaller than ~48 × 48 dp are frequently
  missed or require multiple attempts
- **Swipe gestures** — back-swipe and pull-to-refresh are not reliably
  discovered; elders expect labelled buttons
- **Autocorrect** — causes significant frustration when typing in Telugu or
  Hindi using English transliteration; leads to avoidance of typing entirely
- **Fear of "breaking" the phone** — elders often freeze rather than try an
  unfamiliar action; undo and confirmation dialogs are high value
- **Small text** — default system font sizes are frequently too small;
  elders often hold the phone at arm's length
- **Notification overload** — multiple apps with badges cause anxiety; elders
  often ignore or dismiss everything
- **Charging anxiety** — low-battery warnings interrupt tasks; elders
  associate low battery with phone "dying"
- **App switching** — returning to a previous app after being interrupted
  (e.g., a call during onboarding) is a common failure point
- **Icon-only navigation** — unlabelled icons are not reliably recognised;
  text labels alongside icons reduce errors significantly
- **Language mismatch** — system-level language set to English when the
  elder's literacy is in Telugu causes pervasive confusion

### SRS implications (to be confirmed per interview)
- Minimum touch target: 48 × 48 dp (Material Design / WCAG 2.5.5)
- Text size: minimum 18 sp body, 22 sp for primary actions
- All navigation items must carry both icon and label
- Swipe should be supplemented by explicit back/forward buttons
- All destructive actions must have a confirmation step
- Onboarding must survive a mid-flow phone call interruption and resume
- Voice message must be one-tap record, one-tap send — no hold-to-record
- App must function correctly when system language is English but content
  language is set to Telugu in-app
- TTS (text-to-speech) for incoming messages is a high-priority accessibility
  feature, not a nice-to-have
